TGIF: Multimodal Temporal Segmentation

Summer – Sep 2026

Summer Research, CEE, Carnegie Mellon University · Advisor: Prof. Pingbo Tang

Multimodal (video + vibration) temporal action segmentation, where Dynamic Window Attention adapts the sensor window to the predicted action segment instead of a fixed length, sharpening attention-based fusion and improving segmentation accuracy.

  • Dual-ROI VideoMAE features with MS-TCN segmentation raised activity recognition from about 80% to 91.9% validation accuracy.
  • DWA fusion reached 92.5% mean accuracy (93.7% best seed) and lifted the Edit score from 89.7% to 91.4%.
  • On WEAR, concatenated Macro-F1 improved from 75.73% to 81.09% over video-only, early-concatenation, and fixed-window baselines.
GitHub Manuscript

Autonomous Delivery Robot System

Jan – May 2026

Independent Study, CEE, Carnegie Mellon University · Advisor: Prof. Pingbo Tang

A Unity-based multi-floor construction-site delivery robot system, connecting semantic topology, Theta* planning, capacity-constrained multi-trip scheduling, and Pure Pursuit execution, with a separate SAC study of local recovery.

  • Multi-trip, multi-commodity routing model with trip-indexed routes, explicit unloading variables, and bounded trip counts that constrain the CBC search space.
  • Three-floor delivery across ramps and an elevator completed 100/100 randomised simulated deliveries at a mean round-trip time of 223 s.
  • PARS, a SAC-based local recovery policy, reached 93.7 ± 2.3% success in the final training interval.

Task-Aware Anchor-Delta Representations for Robot Learning ongoing

Sep 2026 – present

Ongoing research in robot learning

Task-aware anchor-delta representations for robot learning: sparse visual anchors plus task-relevant state deltas in place of dense video. The question is whether manipulation policies need continuous dense observation at all, or whether anchors and deltas preserve the decision-relevant information at a fraction of the storage.
