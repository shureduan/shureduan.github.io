Video + physical signals · Temporal action segmentation

Dynamic Window Attention
for Multimodal Activity Understanding

What is a person doing, and when does the activity change?

We combine video with vibration or wearable motion signals to recognize activities over time. DWA adapts the sensor attention window to local signal structure, helping the model use the relevant physical context around each action.

03 / The idea

Let the signal determine how far to attend.

A fixed window may include an unrelated quiet interval or miss part of a longer action. Our latest WEAR implementation describes the signal’s coarse shape, resizes the window, and uses video to attend to the selected sensor interval.

  1. 1

    Describe the signal

    AC-RMS envelopes capture local activity strength. Ordered slope histograms describe whether the signal is rising, flat or falling, while preserving its rough temporal shape.

  2. 2

    Resize the window

    Starting from a local interval or a predicted action segment, DWA keeps a contiguous region with similar signal structure. It contracts at changes and expands through similar neighbors.

  3. 3

    Attend and refine

    Video queries attend to raw sensor keys and values within that window. A shared MS-TCN refines the action timeline over two rounds, followed by background calibration.

Signal shape selects the context; raw sensor features provide the evidence.

The descriptor controls window length. The fusion direction remains video → sensor, so the temporal support can change without replacing the sensor information used by attention.
